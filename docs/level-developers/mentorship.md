# Mentorship Guide

## Overview
Mentorship is crucial for developer growth at all levels. This guide covers both being a mentee and being a mentor.

## For Mentees

### Finding a Mentor
- Look for someone 1-2 levels above you
- Consider your growth areas
- Seek someone with complementary skills
- Find someone with time to invest

### Making the Ask
```
Hi [Name],

I really admire your work on [specific project/skill]. I'm looking to improve my 
[specific skill area], and I think I could learn a lot from you.

Would you be open to a mentorship relationship? I'm thinking we could meet 
[frequency] for [duration] to discuss [topics].

I'm committed to making good use of your time and coming prepared to our sessions.

Thanks for considering!
```

### Mentorship Sessions

#### Before Each Session
- [ ] Prepare questions/topics
- [ ] Review previous action items
- [ ] Bring specific examples
- [ ] Set session goals

#### During Session
- [ ] Share updates on progress
- [ ] Discuss challenges
- [ ] Ask for advice
- [ ] Take notes
- [ ] Define action items

#### After Session
- [ ] Review notes
- [ ] Complete action items
- [ ] Follow up on resources
- [ ] Reflect on learnings

### Topics to Cover
- Code review of your work
- Career planning
- Technical skill development
- Soft skills improvement
- Industry insights
- Work-life balance
- Political navigation

### Being a Good Mentee
- Be prepared for sessions
- Follow through on commitments
- Respect their time
- Be open to feedback
- Share your progress
- Ask thoughtful questions

## For Mentors

### Responsibilities
- Provide guidance and advice
- Share experiences
- Review mentee's work
- Connect mentee with opportunities
- Be available for questions
- Challenge mentee to grow

### Effective Mentoring

#### First Session
1. Get to know mentee
   - Background
   - Current role
   - Goals
   - Challenges

2. Set expectations
   - Meeting frequency
   - Communication channels
   - Session format
   - Duration of relationship

3. Create development plan
   - Identify goals
   - Define success metrics
   - Set milestones
   - Plan learning path

#### Regular Sessions
1. **Review progress** (10 min)
   - What's going well?
   - What's challenging?
   - Action item updates

2. **Deep dive topic** (30 min)
   - Code review
   - Skill development
   - Problem solving
   - Career guidance

3. **Action planning** (10 min)
   - Next steps
   - Resources to explore
   - Goals for next session

### Mentoring Techniques

#### Socratic Method
Ask questions rather than give answers:
- "What have you tried?"
- "What do you think the issue is?"
- "How would you approach this?"
- "What are the tradeoffs?"

#### Code Review
- Focus on teaching, not just fixing
- Explain the "why" behind suggestions
- Share patterns and principles
- Celebrate good decisions

#### Career Guidance
- Share your career journey
- Discuss decision-making frameworks
- Connect with opportunities
- Provide honest feedback

### Common Mentee Challenges

#### Imposter Syndrome
- Normalize the feeling
- Share your experiences
- Focus on progress, not perfection
- Celebrate wins

#### Technical Struggles
- Break problems into smaller parts
- Provide resources
- Pair program together
- Review fundamentals

#### Career Uncertainty
- Explore options together
- Share different paths
- Help clarify values
- Support decision-making

### Time Management
- Schedule regular sessions
- Set clear boundaries
- Use async communication
- Batch similar requests
- Know when to delegate

### Measuring Success
- Mentee skill growth
- Increased confidence
- Career progression
- Positive feedback
- Relationship quality

## Mentorship Programs

### 1:1 Mentorship
- One mentor, one mentee
- Deep, personalized guidance
- Flexible structure
- Long-term relationship

### Group Mentorship
- One mentor, multiple mentees
- Shared learning
- Peer support
- Efficient for mentor

### Peer Mentorship
- Similar-level developers
- Mutual learning
- Accountability partnership
- Skill exchange

### Reverse Mentorship
- Junior mentors senior
- Fresh perspectives
- New technology transfer
- Inclusive culture

## Program Structure

### Duration
- **Short-term:** 3 months (specific goal)
- **Medium-term:** 6 months (skill development)
- **Long-term:** 1 year+ (career guidance)

### Frequency
- **Weekly:** Intensive learning
- **Bi-weekly:** Standard cadence
- **Monthly:** Maintenance/guidance

### Format
- **Scheduled meetings:** Core sessions
- **Async chat:** Quick questions
- **Code reviews:** Practical feedback
- **Shadowing:** Learn by observing
